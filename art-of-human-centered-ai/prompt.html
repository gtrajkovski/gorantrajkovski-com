The Art of Human-Centered Prompt Engineering

Where the trilogy begins: the craft of asking well. A single exchange between a person and a model — and why the so-called magic words stopped mattering once the machines got good.

Buy on Amazon ← Back to the trilogy

What it's about

Asking well.

In 2023, everyone was hunting for magic words — the perfect incantation that would unlock a model's best answer. This book was written in that moment, and against it. The premise: prompting is not a bag of tricks but a design discipline, closer to writing a good brief or asking a good question than to casting a spell.

It introduces a human-centered way to think about a single exchange — how structure, meaning, and form shape what comes back — and why, as models improved, the craft shifted from clever wording toward clarity of intent. It is the foundation the rest of the trilogy builds on: before context, before agency, there was the asking.

The framework

The HINT framework

Three pillars of prompt mastery — the architecture of the ask, the substance beneath the words, and the experience of the exchange.

The goal

Human-centered design

Use these pillars to turn transactional commands into meaningful, collaborative human-AI interactions.
