The reframe
The frontier of artificial intelligence is no longer the question of what individual models can do. The frontier is the infrastructure inside which judgment forms — the predictive scaffolding through which everyday cognitive work is now performed, the platforms on which that scaffolding sits, and the institutions that have not yet developed the categories required to govern it.

§ 06  ·  A scaffold, in operation

Type a sentence. Notice the next phrase being offered.

The book's central technical object is the adaptive predictive scaffold: a system that doesn't replace your thinking, it precedes it. Each phrase you might write arrives already drafted; your work shifts from composition to selection. Over years, the developmental experience of writing alone is replaced by the experience of writing alongside something that anticipated the next move.

The question Chapter Three asks is not whether the scaffold is useful. It is. The question is what becomes of the cognitive practice that the scaffold was supposed to support, once the scaffold becomes the medium in which the practice is performed.
