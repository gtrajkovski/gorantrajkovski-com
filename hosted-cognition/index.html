The reframe
The frontier of artificial intelligence is no longer the question of what individual models can do. The frontier is the infrastructure inside which judgment forms — the predictive scaffolding through which everyday cognitive work is now performed, the platforms on which that scaffolding sits, and the institutions that have not yet developed the categories required to govern it.
