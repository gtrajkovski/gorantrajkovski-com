How I work

Three steps. No theatrics.

  1. 1.

    Discovery

    Three questions answered before anything else: What's the objective? Why now? What's the most efficient path? If we can't answer these, we're not ready to start — and I'll tell you that.

  2. 2.

    Plan

    Logical, effective plans grounded in facts and reality — not aspiration. Every practical step identified. You'll see exactly what gets done, by whom, and when. Timelines force closure; closure forces value.

  3. 3.

    Ship

    Analysis to action with essentially zero gap. I think and act simultaneously — it's the operating model that lets one person do the work of a team. Velocity is the whole point.

About

The Architect.

Builder. Teacher. Maker. Architect of things that did not exist before.

I came to artificial intelligence in the mid-1990s, when it was an academic backwater most computer scientists treated as a curiosity. I did not pivot when the market told me to. I was already there, doing the work, when nobody was paying attention.

I think and act simultaneously. Before proceeding I need three questions answered: What is the objective? Why are we doing this? What is the most efficient way of doing this? Once those are answered, there is no stopping me.

Direct AND warm. Rigorous AND fast. Prolific AND deep. The contradictions aren't a problem — they're how the work gets shipped.
