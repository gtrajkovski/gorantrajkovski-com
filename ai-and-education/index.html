Program 01 · Available now

AI and Assessment
in Higher Education.

A practical program that helps your faculty redesign assessments to produce trustworthy evidence of learning, in any discipline. Your faculty don't need another AI policy. They need to know their assessments still mean something. This gives them a way.

This is an evidence problem, not an integrity problem.

Every grade your institution awards is a claim that a student learned something, and those claims flow into transcripts, program reviews, and the degrees you confer. All of it rests on one assumption: that the work was the student's own thinking.

Generative AI quietly broke that assumption, and detection tools and AI policies don't repair it. They manage behavior. They don't restore the evidence.

What's at stake isn't a handful of misconduct cases. It's whether your assessments still support the claims your credentials make.
